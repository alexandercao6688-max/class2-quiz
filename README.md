# class2-quiz

NZ Class 2 learner theory practice quiz (installable web app).

**Live site:** https://alexandercao6688-max.github.io/class2-quiz/

Practice only. Always study the official NZ Road Code and Heavy Vehicle Road Code. Questions are based on publicly available NZTA information. Official wording and images should not be copied.

## Use it online or offline

Open the live site in your browser. After the first visit, the quiz files are stored on your device and the app should still load without an internet connection.

## Add to your iPhone or iPad home screen

1. Open the [live site](https://alexandercao6688-max.github.io/class2-quiz/) in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## Add to your Android home screen

1. Open the [live site](https://alexandercao6688-max.github.io/class2-quiz/) in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu (three dots).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Files

- `index.html` — the quiz, with home-screen and offline support
- `class2-theory-quiz.html` — the same quiz, kept under the original filename
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icons/`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, and `favicon.ico` — install and offline support
